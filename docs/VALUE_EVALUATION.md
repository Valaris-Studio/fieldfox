# Evaluating value without hiding review work

This is a proposed evaluation method, not evidence of measured savings. A deterministic provider can validate the widget/server integration; it cannot establish extraction quality, human time saved, adoption or willingness to pay.

Choose one repetitive form and a person who actually completes it. For an initial owned-fixture study, the workshop signup in examples/plain-html/index.html gives a bounded task: transfer an inscription into name, contact, date, size, session, interests and notes, then review it. Leave the final Submit click to the person. Native controls in this form are the scope of that study; the React/RHF, Radix, ARIA and editor fixtures establish separate technical coverage.

Before observing results, close a case manifest and expected answers. One proposed exploratory sample is 50 synthetic cases: 20 text, 15 images and 15 PDF. Each modality includes complete, absent, ambiguous, differently formatted and previously populated data. An ambiguous value should be omitted. Use only authorized source material and provider access. Synthetic data does not establish representativeness for real operations.

Pair equivalent variants and alternate manual/assisted order. Measure total elapsed time, including preparation, provider wait, correction and final review. Record abandonment, omitted fields, assistance and important errors both before and after review. Never discard a failed attempt or subtract review time to improve the result. Keep form contents, source documents and answers outside cloud analytics.

For completed pairs, calculate 1 - assisted_total/manual_total and report the median paired reduction by modality alongside every attempt's completion/abandonment counts. A proposed >=30% saving is subject to process-owner ratification, together with zero automatic submissions and no increase in important final errors. The sample is exploratory, not a statistical certification or a promise about all forms.

Observe adoption separately: a developer who did not build Fieldfox follows the exact guide with an identified artifact, installs it on an owned page and repeats on a second form. Count every intervention. A local tarball and local endpoint prove experimental distribution, not public CDN availability. Test self-hosted with the same widget bytes and its own backend, without a cloud account.

The process owner decides the next bounded pilot from evidence. A second automated run is not retention, praise is not a purchase, and failed evidence does not silently change the hosted-first or permanently supported MIT self-hosted decisions.
